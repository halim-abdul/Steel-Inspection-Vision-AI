# Predictive Maintenance

The maintenance track estimates machine health and remaining useful life (RUL) from vibration, temperature, acoustic, current, speed and process variables. The research protocol separates early warning, fault diagnosis and RUL estimation. Evaluation uses MAE/RMSE for RUL, PR-AUC for rare failures, calibration, lead time, false alarms per operating hour and cost-sensitive utility.

Data splits must be asset-aware and time-aware. No future windows may leak into training.
