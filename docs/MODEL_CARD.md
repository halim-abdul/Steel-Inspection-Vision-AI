# Model Card

## Intended use
Research on steel surface inspection, equipment-condition monitoring and predictive-maintenance decision support.

## Not intended for
Unvalidated autonomous safety decisions or plant control without engineering review.

## Key risks
Domain shift across plants, steel grades, illumination systems and camera/sensor configurations; rare-defect imbalance; uncertain labels; sensor calibration errors; false confidence outside the training distribution.

## Reporting
Always include dataset provenance, split logic, class frequencies, per-class metrics, uncertainty/calibration, latency hardware and known failure modes.
