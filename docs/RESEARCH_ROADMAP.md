# Research Roadmap

## Phase A — strong baselines
Establish reproducible YOLOv8 detection, classical sensor anomaly baselines and RUL regression.

## Phase B — robustness
Study cross-coil and cross-grade generalization, illumination shift, calibration, rare defects and sensor drift.

## Phase C — multimodal learning
Compare late fusion against temporal gating, sequence encoders and cross-attention.

## Phase D — industrial validation
Profile line-side latency, false alarms per shift, quality cost and maintenance lead time.

## Phase E — publication package
Freeze splits, seeds and evaluation scripts; include confidence intervals, ablations, failure analysis and model/data cards.
